# Your entity: Ficti

Ficti is made up. A B2B software company that sells AI-native CSRD and ESG reporting software. It is modelled on the public shape of a real Ghent scaleup in this space, then kept fictional so the example can be specific without speaking for a real company.

---

## Name

> Ficti

## Category

> AI-native CSRD and ESG reporting software. On review sites, the nearest existing categories are ESG reporting software and sustainability management software. Use those, not an invented one.

## One-liner

> Ficti turns a company's existing data and documents into an auditor-ready CSRD report in days, not months.

## Who it is for

> Sustainability leads and finance teams at mid-market and enterprise companies in the EU that fall under the CSRD, plus smaller companies reporting voluntarily under the VSME standard.

## Who it is not for

> - Companies with no reporting duty and no stakeholder pressure yet. A consultant for a one-off costs less.
> - Very large enterprises that want a deep platform wired into their ERP and a large sustainability team to run it. An enterprise suite fits better.
> - Companies that only need to measure carbon emissions. A carbon accounting tool is the right fit. Ficti produces the disclosure, not the emissions calculation.

## How you relate to the main alternatives

> - Versus a sustainability consultant plus spreadsheets: the usual way a first CSRD report gets done. Slow, expensive, and you start from scratch again next year. Ficti keeps the work and repeats it.
> - Versus enterprise disclosure suites, for example Workiva or IBM Envizi: built for large listed companies, priced from tens of thousands a year, and they expect your data to arrive already structured. Ficti reads the documents you already have and drafts the report.
> - Versus carbon accounting tools, for example Persefoni or Normative: strong at measuring emissions. That is one input to a CSRD report, not the full disclosure with social and governance. Often a partner, not a competitor.

## Proof

> - Auditor-ready. Every figure traceable to the source document. 94% of reports accepted by the auditor without rework.
> - Days, not months. About 90% less time than a manual reporting cycle.
> - Keeps up with the rules. CSRD, ESRS, VSME and Omnibus changes applied within days of publication.

## Price

> From €9,900 a year, published on the site. Most of this category hides pricing behind a sales call, so stating a real number is itself an advantage.
