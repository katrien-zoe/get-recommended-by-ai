# Questions buyers ask: Ficti

The questions a sustainability lead or a finance person types into an AI when they need to produce a CSRD or VSME report. Ficti cannot win the broad category question, where the large enterprise suites dominate. It can win the use-case and alternatives questions, where a mid-market buyer is closer to a decision.

---

## Category questions

> - What is the best CSRD reporting software?
> - What software helps a company produce an ESG report?

*Workiva, Watershed and the other large platforms own these today. Ficti competes here only over time, and should not spend its first effort chasing them.*

## Use-case questions

> - What software produces an auditor-ready CSRD report from our existing data?
> - How can a mid-market company do CSRD without a six-figure enterprise contract?
> - What is the best VSME reporting tool for an SME?
> - CSRD reporting software that reads our documents instead of manual data entry?

*This is the focus. Each one points at something Ficti actually does better.*

## Comparison questions

> - Workiva versus a dedicated CSRD tool for a mid-market company?
> - Consultant versus software for a first CSRD report?

## Alternatives questions

> - What are the alternatives to hiring a consultant for CSRD reporting?
> - What are the alternatives to an enterprise suite for a company that is not a large enterprise?

*Ficti's best opening. These buyers already want a lighter option.*

## Objection questions

> - Can software produce a CSRD report that an auditor will accept?
> - How do we keep up with changing CSRD and Omnibus rules?
