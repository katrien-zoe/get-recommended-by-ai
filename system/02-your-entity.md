# Your entity

One clear description of your company, used the same way everywhere. This is the single most useful file in the repo. Fill it in, then reuse it word for word on your site, your review profiles, your LinkedIn page, and anywhere else a source might describe you.

The goal is that every place an AI reads describes you the same way. When the description matches across the web, the AI treats you as one clear thing. When it does not, the AI leaves you out or gets you wrong.

Replace the text in each field. Keep it short.

---

## Name

The exact company and product name, written one way. Decide capitalisation and spacing now and never vary it.

> [Your name]

## Category

The category a buyer would put you in. Use a term that already exists and that review sites use, not one you invented.

> [Your category]

## One-liner

One sentence. What you do and who it is for. No adjectives, no hype.

> [What you do, for whom]

## Who it is for

The buyer you fit. Be specific about size, type, and situation.

> [Your buyer]

## Who it is not for

The buyer you do not fit, and who should pick something else. Saying this plainly makes the AI trust the rest, and keeps the wrong buyers from arriving and leaving a bad review.

> [Who should not buy]

## How you relate to the main alternatives

Name the real alternatives, including the status quo. For each, say in one line what the difference is. Use the competitors' real names. Buyers and AI already know them.

> - Versus [competitor]: [the difference in one line]
> - Versus [competitor]: [the difference in one line]
> - Versus doing nothing / the manual way: [the difference in one line]

## Proof

Two or three facts a source could quote, each with a number or a name attached. Price, a specific capability, who built it, a result.

> - [Fact with a number]
> - [Fact with a number]
> - [Fact with a name]

## Price

The real price, as a number if you can. AI often gets software pricing wrong, which frustrates buyers. Stating it clearly helps the AI get it right.

> [Your price]
