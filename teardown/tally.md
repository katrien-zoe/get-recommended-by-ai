# Teardown: why AI recommends Tally

This one is real, and you can go and check it yourself.

Ficti shows the journey, a company starting from zero with a plan. Tally shows the destination, a real company that AI already recommends. This is not a plan for Tally, and not advice aimed at them. It is a read of why they win, so you can see the signals from `01-how-ai-recommends.md` working in a real case.

Tally is a Ghent-based form builder, known for a generous free plan and a Notion-style editor. Ask an AI "best free form builder" or "Typeform alternatives" and Tally almost always comes up.

## What AI says today

As of mid-2026, Tally is named in nearly every "best form builder", "Typeform alternative" and "Google Forms alternative" guide, and it is the tool other builders define themselves against, so there are lists titled "best Tally alternatives" too. Independent AI-visibility analyses report that Perplexity regularly surfaces Tally in "best free" and "Typeform alternative" answers, and that it is treated as a top pick for individual users because almost everything is free.

You do not have to take that on trust. Ask a few engines the questions above and watch.

## Why it works, mapped to the signals

| Signal | What Tally has |
|---|---|
| Named by many independent sources | It is in almost every form-builder buyer guide, and the reference point others compare against. Breadth is high. |
| A consistent description everywhere | The same three things follow it around: free, unlimited responses, Notion-style editor. One clear entity. |
| A quotable hook | "Almost everything free" is a single, sticky claim an AI can lift and repeat. It owns the free position. |
| Present where its buyers are | Its buyers are indie makers and startup people, who are on Reddit, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt and X. All public, all indexable. |
| An AI-native surface of its own | Tally runs an MCP server, so ChatGPT and Claude can read its form data and edit forms. That gets it written about in "AI plus forms" pieces. |
| Review-site presence | Profiles and strong ratings sit under the trust layer, even when they are not the visible citation. |

## The lesson

Two things stand out.

First, this is the same lesson as Ficti, from the other side. Ficti's buyers are not on Reddit, so its plan avoids it. Tally's buyers are on Reddit, so its presence there is part of why AI knows it. Same rule, opposite answer: go where your buyers actually are.

Second, the footprint that grew Tally is the footprint AI now reads. It grew through a free tier and word of mouth on Product Hunt, Reddit and X. That public trail is exactly what an AI gathers when it recommends a form builder. The growth engine and the AI recommendation run on the same thing.

## Honest limits

Being recommended is not permanent, for anyone. An AI answer reflects the current public consensus, and consensus moves. New tools appear, rivals publish their own comparison pages, and the sources an AI reads get rewritten. So the "best for X" answer this year is not guaranteed next year.

The point for your own company is the useful part. Getting recommended is not a finish line. It is a position you hold by keeping the footprint current, the same work that earned it in the first place.
