# example/

The system in this repo, filled in for a made-up company.

**Ficti is made up.** A B2B software company that sells AI-native CSRD and ESG reporting software, sold to sustainability leads and finance teams at companies that fall under EU sustainability rules. It is modelled on the public shape of a real Ghent scaleup in this space, then kept fictional so the example can be specific without speaking for a real company.

## What this example shows that the generic advice does not

Most AEO advice tells everyone to build a Reddit presence. Ficti's buyers are not on Reddit. They are on LinkedIn, in industry groups, and they lean on ESG consultants and auditors.

So the Ficti plan puts its community effort where those buyers actually are, and turns consultant and auditor trust into public, indexable proof. The lesson is that the source plan has to match where your buyers are, not where a checklist assumes they are.

For the opposite case, a company whose buyers really are on Reddit, see the Tally teardown in [`../teardown/tally.md`](../teardown/tally.md).
