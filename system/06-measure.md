# Measure

A monthly check that shows whether any of this is working. You can do it by hand in about an hour. Paid tools exist for this, but you do not need one to start.

## The method

1. Take your prompt set from `03-questions-buyers-ask.md`.
2. Once a month, run each prompt in the main AI engines your buyers use. As of mid-2026 that is usually ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI mode.
3. For each prompt and each engine, record four things:
   - Are you named? Yes or no.
   - If named, where in the list? First, top few, or buried.
   - Is the description accurate? Note anything wrong, especially the price.
   - Which sources are cited, and which competitors are named?
4. Keep the results. The point is the trend over months, not any single answer.

Run the same prompts each month, in a fresh chat with no memory of earlier questions, so the months are comparable.

## What to watch

- **Presence.** The share of your prompts where you are named at all. This usually moves first, and it moves once your review profiles are live.
- **Share of voice.** How often you are named compared with your main competitors, across the whole set.
- **Accuracy.** Whether the AI describes you correctly. A wrong price or a wrong category is a problem to fix at the source.
- **Sources.** Which pages the AI leans on. These tell you where to put effort next.

## A note on speed

Expect the retrieval-based engines, Perplexity and Google's AI mode, to reflect changes first, within days or weeks of new reviews or pages. The chat models change more slowly. If presence rises on the fast engines and not yet on the others, that is normal, not a failure.
