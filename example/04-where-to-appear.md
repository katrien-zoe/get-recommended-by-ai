# Where to appear: Ficti

Filled in for Ficti. The order matters, and the community part is deliberately different from the standard advice, because Ficti's buyers are not on Reddit.

## 1. Review sites

Ficti is not on any review site yet. That alone is enough to keep it out of most AI answers. This is the first job.

Action:
- Create profiles on Capterra and G2 under ESG reporting software and sustainability management software. Exact name, one-liner and €9,900 starting price from `02-your-entity.md`.
- Ask the customers whose reports passed audit for a review. The sustainability lead who got signed off is the person to ask.
- Part of Ficti's buyer is enterprise, and that end of the market also reads analyst sources like Gartner Peer Insights and the Verdantix Green Quadrant. Those take longer to earn. Start with G2 and Capterra now, and aim at analyst coverage as Ficti grows.

## 2. A consistent description everywhere

Ficti is described a few different ways across its site and its founders' posts. Pick one and use it everywhere.

Action:
- Put the `02-your-entity.md` one-liner and the €9,900 price on the website, the review profiles, the LinkedIn page and any directory. Same words each time.
- Publish the price. Most of this category hides it, and AI often gets ESG software pricing wrong. A clear number is both a buyer service and an advantage.

## 3. Third-party lists and comparison pages

There are "best CSRD reporting software" and "best ESG reporting software" buyer guides that an AI reads. Some of them now structure their data specifically for AI tools. Ficti is in none of them.

Action:
- Find the buyer guides that already rank for CSRD and ESG software and ask to be included, with the honest one-liner and the price.
- Publish honest comparison pages: Ficti versus an enterprise suite, Ficti versus a consultant, Ficti versus a carbon tool. Say plainly where each of them is the better choice.
- Aim for several independent sources describing Ficti the same way. Breadth matters.

## 4. Communities where buyers actually talk

This is where Ficti differs from the standard playbook. The standard advice says build a Reddit presence. For Ficti that is close to a waste of time.

Ficti's buyers are sustainability leads, CFOs and finance people. They are on LinkedIn, in industry associations and sustainability networks, and they lean heavily on ESG consultants and auditors. They are not debating CSRD tools on Reddit.

Action:
- Do not force a Reddit presence. It is not where these buyers are.
- Put that effort into LinkedIn, where these buyers actually read, and into being known by the ESG consultants and auditors who shape the shortlist. Their write-ups and recommendations are also sources an AI reads.
- Turn customer results into public, indexable proof: named case studies, a talk at an industry event written up online, a mention in a consultant's guide.

## 5. Own pages

Covered in the output file. The most useful pages for Ficti are a Ficti versus enterprise-suite page, a Ficti versus consultant page, a VSME page for smaller companies, a plain answer to "will an auditor accept this", and a pricing page with the real number on it.
