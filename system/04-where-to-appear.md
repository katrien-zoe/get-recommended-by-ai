# Where to appear

The sources AI reads when it recommends software, in rough order of weight for B2B, with what to do about each. Most of the work in this repo is here, off your own site.

Do these in order. The first two are close to required. The rest raise where you sit once you are included.

## 1. Review sites

The inclusion gate. If you are not on the main review sites, most AI answers leave you out before they start.

For B2B software the ones that matter most are G2 and Capterra, which are now the same company after G2 acquired Capterra in early 2026. TrustRadius is worth adding. Gartner Peer Insights only matters if you sell to enterprise IT and your category has Magic Quadrant coverage.

Action:
- Claim and complete your profiles. Exact name from `02-your-entity.md`, correct category, one-liner, logo, screenshots, real price.
- Get reviews from real customers, and keep them coming. Recency matters more than a big one-time push. Ask the customers who use you most and got a result.
- Do not buy or fake reviews. They get removed and they put the profile at risk.
- A high star rating helps trust but does not decide your place. Do not chase the average.

## 2. A consistent description everywhere

The AI needs to see the same you in every source. This costs little and is often skipped.

Action:
- Use the `02-your-entity.md` name, category, and one-liner word for word on your site, your review profiles, your LinkedIn page, your Crunchbase entry, and anywhere else you are listed.
- Fix old pages and directories that describe you a different way.
- If you are notable enough for a Wikipedia entry and the facts support one, it is a strong, neutral source. Do not force it.

## 3. Third-party lists and comparison pages

The "best tool for X" articles and "X versus Y" pages that other people publish. AI reads these heavily.

Action:
- Find the lists that already rank for your category and use case. Ask to be included, with a clear, honest description they can use.
- Where a fair comparison does not exist, help create one, through a guest piece, a podcast, or a partner.
- Aim for several independent sources describing you. Breadth matters. Being named across four or more places raises your odds noticeably.

## 4. Communities where your buyers actually talk

User-generated discussion carries weight, because AI treats it as real people rather than marketing. But only the communities where your buyers actually are.

Action:
- Find where your buyers discuss this. For many B2B categories that is Reddit. For a narrow trade it may be a specific forum, a Slack group, or an industry association.
- Be useful there as a person, over time. Answer questions, do not drop links.
- If your buyers are not in public, indexable places, do not force a Reddit presence for its own sake. Put the effort into the sources above instead.

## 5. Your own pages

Read late by the AI, but still read, and fully in your control. Covered in `05-make-your-pages-answerable.md`.
